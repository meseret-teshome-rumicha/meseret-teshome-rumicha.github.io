Module 1 · Sentence elements

Identifying Subject and Predicate

Underline the complete subject once and the complete predicate twice. Then enter the simple subject and simple predicate (verb).
A.The diligent university students researched in the library all afternoon.
Suggested answer: Simple subject: students. Simple predicate: researched.
B.Several historical documents on the desk belong to the archives department.
Suggested answer: Simple subject: documents. Simple predicate: belong. Words inside the prepositional phrase “on the desk” are not part of the simple subject.
Module 2 · Clause analysis

Defining and Non-Defining Relative Clauses

Identify each relative clause as defining (essential) or non-defining (non-essential). Add commas where necessary.
A.The professor who teaches computer science won an international research award.
Suggested answer: Defining (essential). No commas are needed because the clause identifies which professor.
B.My primary reference book which was published in 2021 contains detailed case studies.
Suggested answer: Non-defining (non-essential). My primary reference book, which was published in 2021, contains detailed case studies.
Module 3 · Functional types

Types of Sentences by Function

Classify each sentence as declarative, interrogative, imperative, or exclamatory. Insert the correct end punctuation.
A.Submit your final project draft by Friday at midnight ___
Suggested answer: Imperative — Submit your final project draft by Friday at midnight.
B.How remarkable this groundbreaking scientific discovery is ___
Suggested answer: Exclamatory — How remarkable this groundbreaking scientific discovery is!
C.Did the research team complete the data analysis yesterday ___
Suggested answer: Interrogative — Did the research team complete the data analysis yesterday?
D.Renewable energy resources play a vital role in modern infrastructure ___
Suggested answer: Declarative — Renewable energy resources play a vital role in modern infrastructure.
Module 4 · Structural types

Types of Sentences by Structure

Identify each sentence as simple, compound, complex, or compound-complex.
A.Although the exam was challenging, Daniel prepared thoroughly, and he achieved the highest grade.
Suggested answer: Compound-complex: two independent clauses and one dependent clause.
B.The author explained her core hypothesis during the opening keynote session.
Suggested answer: Simple: one independent clause.
C.The lab assistant cleaned the equipment, and the researcher recorded the daily metrics.
Suggested answer: Compound: two independent clauses joined by the coordinating conjunction “and.”
D.Because the rain was continuous, the field study was postponed until next week.
Suggested answer: Complex: one dependent clause and one independent clause.

Puzzle 1 · Sentence mechanics

Subject and Predicate Division

For each sentence: (1) draw a vertical line (|) between the complete subject and complete predicate; (2) identify the simple subject; and (3) identify the main verb or auxiliary.
A.The rapid advancement of artificial intelligence tools in higher education has transformed traditional assessment methods.
Suggested answer: The rapid advancement of artificial intelligence tools in higher education | has transformed traditional assessment methods. Simple subject: advancement. Main verb phrase: has transformed.
B.Determining the validity of the research methodology took the committee several months.
Suggested answer: Determining the validity of the research methodology | took the committee several months. Simple subject: Determining the validity of the research methodology (the gerund phrase functions as the subject); main verb: took.
C.Among the factors influencing consumer choices, social media advertising remains the most dominant.
Suggested answer: Among the factors influencing consumer choices, social media advertising | remains the most dominant. Simple subject: advertising (within the noun phrase “social media advertising”); main verb: remains.
Puzzle 2 · Defining vs. non-defining

Relative Clause Reconstruction

Combine each pair of statements into one sentence using a relative clause. Use appropriate pronouns and commas.

1. Specific condition (defining clause)

Statement A: The survey respondents were excluded from the final analysis.

Statement B: These respondents submitted incomplete questionnaires.

Suggested answer: The survey respondents who submitted incomplete questionnaires were excluded from the final analysis. “That” is also acceptable, although “who” is preferred for people in academic writing.

2. Extra background context (non-defining clause)

Statement A: Oxford University was founded in the 12th century.

Statement B: Oxford University is one of the oldest higher education institutions.

Suggested answer: Oxford University, which is one of the oldest higher education institutions in the world, was founded in the 12th century.
Puzzle 3 · Functional registers

Academic Sentence Functions

Base idea: “The data contains severe statistical anomalies.”

Rewrite the idea in each required formal academic register.

A. Formal interrogative (research question)

Suggested answer: To what extent do these statistical anomalies compromise the overall validity of the dataset?

B. Academic imperative (research directive)

Suggested answer: Examine the raw data carefully to identify the underlying cause of these statistical anomalies.

C. Formal academic exclamatory (emphatic statement)

Suggested answers: How remarkably inconsistent these dataset results are compared to established baseline figures!
OR: Seldom have researchers encountered such severe statistical anomalies in a controlled study!
Puzzle 4 · Sentence structure

Structural Code-Breaking

Use the sentence blocks to construct the requested sentence structures.
Block 1: Although the economic policy promised inflation reduction…
Block 2: The national currency continued to depreciate…
Block 3: Foreign investors withdrew their capital…

A. Complex sentence (CX): use Blocks 1 and 2

Suggested answer: Although the economic policy promised rapid inflation reduction, the national currency continued to depreciate.

B. Compound-complex sentence (CCX): combine Blocks 1, 2, and 3

Suggested answer: Although the economic policy promised rapid inflation reduction, the national currency continued to depreciate, and foreign investors withdrew their capital from local markets.

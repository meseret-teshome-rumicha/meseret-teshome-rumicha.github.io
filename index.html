Practice activities for university students developing sentence, paragraph, and essay writing skills.

Welcome

Choose a category below to practise writing step by step. Each section contains short activities with feedback, writing prompts, and checklists. Downloadable worksheets are included where available.

Tip: complete the activity, check the feedback, then revise your own writing.

5. Essay Writing

Using these resources

  1. Choose a topic.
  2. Complete the online practice.
  3. Check the feedback and revise your answer.
  4. Download worksheets when available.
  5. Keep a copy of your improved writing in a portfolio.
